# Normandy P4: the German defences

Plan: [docs/NORMANDY_PLAN.md](../NORMANDY_PLAN.md) P4.

## What changed
- `js/coast_defence_data.js` (new): 12 element kinds (88 / 75 / 50 mm casemates, 75 / 76.2 mm field guns, Tobruk,
  MG 42 nest, mortar pit, 2 cm flak, the 155 mm K 418(f), the observation bunker, the anti-tank wall) and 13
  strongpoints: WN 60, 61, 62, 64, 65, 66, 68, 70, 71, 72, 73, 74 and Pointe du Hoc (WN 75), 70 elements, placed on
  the coast by `at` (beach / foot / slope / top), each with the draw it guards.
- `js/coast_defence.js` (new): elements and WN units (duck-typed, never in `WW.world.ships`; ids 9150+ / 9200+).
  - Damage: a casemate hit through its embrasure arc takes 1x and may lose its gun at once (25 / 35 / 50% for
    small / med / big); on its sea wall 0.15x; a bomb on its roof 0.3x; MG rounds nothing.
  - Near misses suppress (6 / 9 / 14 / 16 u, 8-25 s) and blast the open positions.
  - A WN is silenced when every weapon in it is out (event; it leaves the Allied plot).
  - Intel is one aggregate scan: every WN is on the Allied chart, and each WN's observers see ships within 320 u and
    planes within 180 u. A muzzle flash reveals an element for 25 s.
  - Registered with `landSites` (impact, hitTest, scan, flak shooters, roofAt, groundAt).
- `js/coast_defence_fire.js` (new): the guns fire real shells (`WW.combat.fireShell`, muzzle at the element's height)
  inside their arcs, on `WW.landSites.gunTick` (the island batteries' reload cycle, now shared; island_base.js uses
  it with the same draw order). A ship shelling a WN draws its guns (the duel). Suppressed crews hold fire.
  MG cones and mortar zones only mark `active` / `load` for P6 / P7 (`WW.landTargets` hook).
- Settings (`normandy.js` `settings`, URL): `?hoc=casemates` puts the 155s in their pits on the point; the default
  is `moved` (inland in the orchard, unmanned, as the Rangers found them). Also `cloud` and `fw190` for P8.
- `combat.js`: `muzzlePos` reads `ship.muzzleY`; `damage()` and `takeDamage()` carry the projectile as a sixth
  argument (ships ignore it); land units get impact fx in combat.js (WW.damage only dresses ships).
- AI sites that skip the island base now skip land units too (`isLand`): fleet strength, the enemy fleet's centre,
  PT targets, pursuit, the carrier-defence check.
- `js/models_bunker.js` (new, visual): casemates with the thick wall to the sea and the embrasure along the beach,
  sandbag rings, pits, guns; burnt and drooping when knocked out; hit and fire fx.
- `main.js`: the `coastDefence` update slot right after `islandBase`.

## Gate results (2026-10-13)
- `trace_dump.js 1..6 x 400 --cmp base.json`: PASS (identical to main).
- `determinism.js --cross 1,2,3 300 --modes render,browser`: PASS (4ed5c61b7258, b77108c6279e, 5d9de2b44284: the
  same hashes as at P1).
- `sim_behaviour.js --only standard,midway` JSON: midway byte-identical. Standard differed in one metric
  (`air_drops` 62.6 vs 64.5). This is a **pre-existing nondeterminism on main**, not P4: standard seed 2 replayed 5
  times on untouched main diverges at t = 420 s in 2 of 5 runs (P4 0 of 5 in that sample; both differ by luck of the
  draw). The baselines that matched in P1 / P3 were lucky too. Worth its own investigation (a visual-time input
  reaching the sim around then).
- `normandy_probe.js 1,2,3 400`: PASS: 13 WN / 70 elements, German guns fire 67-142 shells per run, the damage
  model unit test (wall 0.15x, embrasure 1x, near-miss suppression, silencing removes the contact).
- `nation_lint.js`: PASS. Screenshots `wn72`, `wn62` reviewed.
