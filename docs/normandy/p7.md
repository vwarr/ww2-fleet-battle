# Normandy P7: infantry

Plan: [docs/NORMANDY_PLAN.md](../NORMANDY_PLAN.md) P7.

## What changed
- `js/infantry_routes.js` (new):
  - The beach zones: water, sand, shingle, shelf, bluff, top.
  - Cover: obstacles on the flat 0.25-0.45; shingle 0.7; the sea wall 0.85; bluff folds 0.5; little in the open sand,
    on the shelf or in a draw.
  - Wire gaps; climb points between the draws (never up one); flank and rear assault points on the top; the rope
    sites at Pointe du Hoc.
- `js/infantry.js` (new): squads of about 12 men as an alive count, gathered from each craft's `craftUnload`.
  - States: wade → cross → shingle (a gap: engineers at 8 s, Rangers 18 s, riflemen 35 s, three times as long while
    pinned) → shelf → climb → top → assault the nearest WN not silenced → hold.
  - The Rangers at Pointe du Hoc go up the ropes (22 s, longer under fire), take WN 75, then find and spike the 155s
    in the orchard.
  - Tanks come ashore as single-vehicle squads; swimmers come from wrecked craft.
  - `WW.landTargets` now gives the defences the craft and the men.
- `js/infantry_fire.js` (new), every 0.25 s, x-bucketed:
  - MG 42 cones fire on the most exposed squad in the cone (and a little on the next): casualties x (1 - cover), range
    fall-off, halved for men lying down; it pins them (suppression).
  - Mortar bombs land in the mortar zones.
  - Squads not pinned and out of the water keep the positions' heads down. An assault kills crews; a crewless
    position is captured. The crews fight back.
  - Tanks fire 75 mm shells (`fireShell`, 'small') along the beach into the embrasures.
  - German shells on the men go through the land_sites hitTest.
- `js/models_infantry.js` (new, visual): up to 3200 instanced soldiers in loose files, prone when pinned or at the
  shingle, bent on the bluff; the fallen stay as bodies (1600); tanks ashore.
- `main.js`: the `infantry` update slot after `landingCraft`; the `infantryFx` frame hook.
