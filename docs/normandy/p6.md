# Normandy P6: landing craft

Plan: [docs/NORMANDY_PLAN.md](../NORMANDY_PLAN.md) P6.

## What changed
- `js/landing_waves.js` (new): the schedule by sector and clock time, at about half the day's craft.
  - H-5: the 741st's DD tanks off Easy Red / Fox Green; the 743rd landed by LCT on Dog.
  - H-hour: the 116th and 16th RCT assault companies.
  - H+3: the engineer gap teams. H+15: the Rangers' C Company at Charlie.
  - H+30: the second wave. H+40: the Rangers' LCAs at Pointe du Hoc.
  - H+50 to H+60: the LCIs. H+80: the 5th Rangers. Then the 18th, 115th and 26th RCT up to 11:30.
  - 100 craft, expanded into one record each with a fixed lane spread (no draws).
- `js/landing_craft.js` (new, sim, Normandy only): LCVP, LCA, LCM, LCT, LCI(L) and DD tank.
  - States: run from the line of departure (420 u out) → beached at the type's draft → ramp → unload (6 men/s, a tank
    every 3 s) → back off and out.
  - The flood current sets craft east (0.3 u/s, the coxswain steers back: they land ~10-28 u east of their lane).
  - The obstacle belts: a mined obstacle wrecks the craft 55% of the time. Coxswains avoid 75% while the obstacles show
    and 20% once the rising tide covers them (H+60). Stakes may hole a craft.
  - Hits from the German guns go through `landSites` hitTest; mortar bombs fall on beached craft in active zones.
  - Wrecks lose a share of the men aboard; the survivors swim for it (`craftUnload` swim).
  - DD tanks swim from 280 u (`?dd=far`, the default) and founder at 0.7%/s in deep water: 7-8 of 8 on the probe
    seeds. `?dd=close` lands them like the 743rd.
  - Not ships: `fleet_cmd`, `intel`, `endgame` and tonnage never see them. `WW.landTargets` lists them for the
    defences, and P7 wraps it.
- `js/models_landing.js` (new, visual): instanced hulls, ramps (up running, down on the sand), LCT tanks, the DD canvas
  screens, settling and burning wrecks, wakes, mine explosions.
- `main.js`: the `landingCraft` update slot after `coastDefence`; the `landingFx` frame hook.
