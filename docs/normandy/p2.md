# Normandy P2: the coast

Plan: [docs/NORMANDY_PLAN.md](../NORMANDY_PLAN.md) P2.

## What changed
- `js/terrain_normandy_data.js` (new): hand-written stations along the coast (waterline z, tidal flat, dry sand,
  shingle, sea wall, marsh shelf, bluff width and height, rock), interpolated along x. Presets: cliff, low cliff,
  beach, beach with sea wall (Vierville to Les Moulins), narrow beach (Charlie, Fox Red). Pointe du Hoc is a run of
  stations jutting the waterline 45 u north with 30 m cliffs. Also the five draws (D-1, D-3, E-1, E-3, F-1), the eight
  sectors (Charlie to Fox Red), seven villages, the four obstacle belts and the Pointe du Hoc crater field.
- `js/terrain_normandy.js` (new): `WW.normandyTerrain.heightRaw` (defined past every map edge), the cross-shore
  profile (sea bed: depth 4.5 at 100 u out, 9.2 at 460 u; tidal flat 0-1.5 over 30-36 u; land: sand, shingle bank,
  sea wall, shelf, bluffs, plateau; draws as notches rising inland; craters on Pointe du Hoc), vertical
  exaggeration `VEX` 1.3 (`?vex=`), ground colours by zone (sand, shingle, marsh, scrub, chalk on the cliffs, a
  patchwork of fields), the obstacles (623 on Omaha, 254 mined; fixed PRNG, per-round `alive`), queries
  (`shoreZ`, `station`, `shingleZ`, `bluffFootZ`, `bluffTopZ`, `sectorAt`).
- `js/terrain_normandy_props.js` (new, visual): villages with churches, hedgerows (the bocage), the sea wall, the
  obstacles (instanced, at ~2x true size like the sailors; re-placed when the sim knocks one down).
- `terrain.js`: a scenario terrain (`scn.terrain`) replaces the islands: no `makeFeatures`, no land-share tuning,
  no palms or huts, `site` null and `padDist` far (no airfield); a colour hook for the floor mesh; coarse floor
  skirts past the south, west and east edges and a water depth texture that covers them (beyond the texture the water
  is drawn deep and opaque and z-fought the land in the 16-bit depth buffer).
- `normandy.js`: the placeholder forces now stand off the real coast (battleships ~460 u out, destroyers 260-300 u).
- Tests: `normandy_probe.js` checks the terrain per seed (depth targets, the flat, the bluff tops, the draws, the
  headland, the same layout on every seed); `tests/normandy_shots.js` (new): overview, fleet, beach, draw, hoc,
  plateau, inland, plot.
