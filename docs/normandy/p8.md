# Normandy P8: air

Plan: [docs/NORMANDY_PLAN.md](../NORMANDY_PLAN.md) P8.

## What changed
- `js/air_offmap.js` (new, sim, Normandy only): planes come from England off the north edge and go back there.
  - They never land, so they live in their own list, not `WW.world.planes`. The carrier air code, CAP, dogfights and
    Pacific AA never see them. The plan's "no carrier home path in aircraft.js" was not needed, and the Pacific air
    code was left untouched.
  - **B-24 boxes** (three boxes of six, 06:05-06:11) level-bomb across the beach with `WW.combat.dropBomb`.
    - With `settings.cloud` (the default, as on the day) the sticks fall ~480 u (3 mi) inland, and no strongpoint
      is hit.
    - With `?cloud=0` they fall among the strongpoints.
  - **P-47 flights** of four arrive from H+60, one every 150 s.
    - Each flight picks a revealed position (or any live one of a strongpoint not silenced) with no friendly squad
      within 30 u.
    - It dives, strafes (`fireShell` 'mg'), releases two bombs each low, pulls out for a second pass and goes home.
  - **Spitfire top cover**: four circling high over the beach, relieved every 220 s. They chase any Fw 190 that comes.
  - **Fw 190s** (`?fw190=1`, flavour: Priller and Wodarczyk strafed Sword and Juno): one low pass along Omaha;
    the men in the path take casualties.
  - The 2 cm flak shoots at planes below 45 u within its range.
- `combat_weapons.js updateBomb`: a bomb bursts on the ground under it (`landSites.groundAt`: the bluff tops in
  Normandy, 0 at sea and in the Pacific, so Pacific bombs are unchanged).
- `js/air_offmap_fx.js` (new, visual): one model per plane from the existing kit (the B-24 as the B-17, the fighters
  as the USN fighter; the Fw 190 in the German palette); shot-down planes fall trailing smoke.
