# Normandy P5: Allied fire support

Plan: [docs/NORMANDY_PLAN.md](../NORMANDY_PLAN.md) P5.

## What changed
- **Classes** (`ship_classes.js`, `only: 'normandy'`): these are forced by key and never in the random class
  lists, so Pacific class picks are unchanged.
  - New York (Texas), Wyoming (Arkansas), Town (HMS Glasgow), La Galissonnière (Montcalm, Georges Leygues),
    Gleaves (nine destroyers) and Hunt III (HMS Talybont).
  - Their guns carry bombardment ranges (`range`: BB 500-560, CL 420, DD 220-260) through `classStats`.
- **Models**: `js/models_normandy_ships.js` (new) adds the old-dreadnought generator (twin turrets, tripod, one
  funnel) and the 6-inch cruiser generator. Gleaves and Hunt reuse the Benson builder.
  - The hull's palette now follows `ship.flag` (`ships.js` buildShip; RN and FNFL greys), and so do the sailors.
- **The order of battle** (`normandy.js` composition): Force O's bombardment group with historical names, flags and
  fire-plan tasks per ship. Satterlee and Talybont support the Rangers at Pointe du Hoc. Two S-boats raid from the
  west as flavour.
  - The compressed clock `N.clock`: 05:50 at t=0, H-hour 06:30 at 60 s, then ~2.6 s per minute.
- `js/ai_fire_support.js` (new): the role for every ship with `ship.fs`, dispatched from `ships_ai.js` update.
  - Pre-planned fire on the charted strongpoints of the ship's tasks.
  - The lift inland at H-5: shells fall on the plateau.
  - Call fire from H+8, only on elements that have shown themselves (muzzle flashes), with spotting (`spottedUntil`).
  - Destroyers close the beach from H+60 (150 s): to 90-120 u, and for a casemate to a spot inside its embrasure arc
    in water at least 4 deep.
  - Heading by the danger field with a risk acceptance of 0.45 before the close and 0.95 after.
  - Bombardment ammunition loads, so the battleships don't run dry.
- `ships_ai.js` guns: a strongpoint target is fired on when it is the ship's fire-support target (it is not an intel
  contact). `ai_endgame.js`: fire-support ships never break for home.
- `main.js` spawn passes `fs` and `name` from the composition (also kept through reposition).
